# Pilot and launch playbook

For Santhoshkumar and Sivasabari. Written for a small team with no budget. Keep every claim to what is true today.

## 1. Pilot clinics (3 to 5)

Who: dentists you already know. Your college's teaching clinic, local clinics, relatives' clinics.
Offer: free for 3 months (matches the landing page: the first 10 clinics get 3 months free, then a locked early price).
In return: a weekly 15-minute call, honest feedback, and a short quote you may use only with their written OK.

Week by week
- Week 0: sit with the dentist and enter 20 real patients together. Import their old list from Excel if they have one.
- Week 1: they run one real day on the app. You sit in or are on WhatsApp.
- Weeks 2 to 4: weekly call. Ask the three questions below. Fix the top problem each week.
- Week 8: ask if they would pay the planned price. Write down the honest answer.

Three questions for each call
1. What did you do on paper or WhatsApp instead of the app this week, and why?
2. What slowed you down?
3. What would make you tell another dentist about it?

Keep a simple sheet: clinic, start date, patients added, bills made, last login, problem list, would they pay (yes, maybe, no).

## 2. Demo script (5 minutes)

1. Add a patient (30 seconds).
2. Odontogram: mark a filling and a missing tooth, slide across teeth to mark several.
3. Treatment plan with prices, then send the link on WhatsApp.
4. Bill with a UPI QR; sell a stock item on the bill.
5. Prescription set (for example After extraction) and a printed letter.
6. Show Data and Backup: download, the daily encrypted off-site backup, archive of old records.
End with: "Which of these do you do by hand today?"

## 3. What to send the lawyer or CA

Ask them to review, and answer in writing:
- Privacy policy and terms pages (the links in the app footer).
- Consent form wording (src/consent.js templates; adapted from IDA model forms).
- Are you a data fiduciary or processor under India's DPDP Act, and what notices and breach steps do you need?
- How long may you keep patient data, and what must happen when a clinic leaves?
- GST: when must you register, and what rate applies to the subscription?
- Do you need a written agreement with each clinic (data processing terms)?
- Is the MeriDent name safe to use and how do you register the trademark?

## 4. Money setup (do when a clinic wants to pay)

- Today a clinic asks for a plan inside the app and you approve it by hand after receiving the payment on your UPI.
- Keep a receipt record for every payment (date, clinic, plan, amount, UPI reference).
- When you want automatic payment, open a Razorpay or Cashfree account (KYC takes a few days) and tell me. I will connect it with the keys kept as environment variables.

## 5. What to say, and what not to say

Say: built by dental students, used in clinics, your data is yours, you can export it any time, encrypted backups run daily.
Do not say: any clinic count, saving, award or approval that is not true and provable today. Do not name another product.

## 6. Support promise

Reply on WhatsApp within one working day. Tell clinics before any price or feature change.
